How placements work

1
Register
Students and companies create their accounts.
2
Apply
Students apply to openings whose CGPA, backlog, department and skill criteria they meet.
3
Shortlist & interview
Companies shortlist applicants and record each interview round.
4
Selection
All rounds passed means selected; any failed round means not selected.
